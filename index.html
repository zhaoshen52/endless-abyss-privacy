Privacy Policy for Endless Abyss Game

Last updated: 2026

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you play our browser game Endless Abyss, published on Playgama platform.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect voluntarily submitted game data:
- Player nickname (for leaderboard display)
- Game score and in-game progress for leaderboard ranking.

We DO NOT collect sensitive personal information: No real name, phone number, email, address, ID card or payment information.

2. Third-Party Advertising

This game uses third-party advertising SDK provided by Playgama.
Advertising partners may collect non-personal technical data such as device type, browser information, anonymous game usage statistics for serving ads.
All data collection follows Playgama privacy rules.

3. Data Storage

Game leaderboard data is stored only for game ranking function. You can reset your in-game nickname in game settings.

4. Cookies

This game may use browser local storage to save your game progress on your device. No tracking cookies for cross-site tracking.
